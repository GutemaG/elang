## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, uplifting, culturally grounded, and playfully energetic identity for gamified language acquisition across Amharic and Afaan Oromo. It avoids generic corporate gamification by fusing tactile, physical-depth UI elements with hand-loomed warmth, rich highland vegetation tones, and sun-baked earthen mineral hues.

The design movement is **Tactile Warm Modernism**:
- **Playful Tactility**: 3D extruded buttons with distinct bottom bevels that compress realistically on press (`active:translate-y-1`), giving learners satisfying, instant physical feedback.
- **Cultural Grounding**: Visual motifs, iconography, and palettes derive from Habesha textiles (Tibeb borders simplified into clean geometric accents), ancient red clay soils, golden highland sunlight, and deep evergreen acacia canopy leaves.
- **Supportive Gamification**: Celebratory elements like streak flames, gem currency, XP bursts, and hearts employ vibrant, luminous accents overlaid on warm cream surfaces rather than harsh stark whites, minimizing screen fatigue and inviting long study streaks.

## Colors

The palette balances historical resonance with high-engagement gaming feedback:

- **Primary (Highland Acacia - `#1B5E3B`)**: Represents growth, lush highlands, and foundational progress. Used for primary learning paths, verified states, and primary lesson headers.
  - Darker shade (`#124027`) serves as the extruded 3D base bevel on primary buttons.
- **Secondary (Simien Gold - `#E08722`)**: Denotes mastery, crowns, lesson completion, and golden chests. Radiates warmth and achievement without eye-straining neon intensity.
  - Bevel shade: `#A85E0E`.
- **Tertiary (Rift Terracotta - `#D84A38`)**: Evokes the iron-rich soil and architectural ochre of Lalibela. Used for gamified hearts, challenge triggers, and urgent practice alerts.
  - Bevel shade: `#9F2B1D`.
- **Gamification Electric Accents**:
  - **Streak Fire / Energy**: `#FF5A1F` with a soft gold outer rim (`#FFA726`).
  - **XP & Gems (Gojjam Emerald & Lapis)**: `#0EA5E9` (Mana/Linguistic Insight) and `#10B981` (Gems).
- **Surfaces & Neutrals**:
  - **Canvas Base (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm Ethiopian parchment/cotton tone that feels natural and gentle on the eyes.
  - **Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean cards with warm beige border accents (`#EFE8DC`).
  - **Surface Sunken (`#F3ECE1`)**: Progress track lanes, inactive paths, and button socket recesses.
  - **Text Primary (`#2B2118`)**: Deep roasted Ethiopian coffee roast, softer and warmer than harsh pitch black.
  - **Text Muted (`#786A5E`)**: Earthen stone taupe for subheaders, timestamps, and phonetic breakdowns.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes approachable warmth, high legibility across multilingual scripts (Latin script for Afaan Oromo / Qubee and Fidel script for Amharic), and structural openness.

- **Primary Font Family**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides wide horizontal proportions, generous apertures, and rounded stroke terminals that pair harmoniously with both Latin alphabets and Amharic Fidel glyph rendering.
- **Ge'ez / Amharic Integration**: For Ethiopic unicode blocks, ensure fallback weights align with the bold presence of Plus Jakarta Sans. Glyphs require extra vertical line-height allowances (+15-20%) compared to Latin text to prevent ascender and descender clipping in vocalic marks.
- **Phonetics & Pronunciation Guides**: Always style IPA and Latin pronunciation transcriptions in `body-sm` with a medium weight (`500`) rendered in `#786A5E` below Fidel script characters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for mobile ergonomics, structured around a fluid, touch-friendly 4-column mobile grid system using dynamic safe padding.

- **Screen Margins**: Fixed `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal safe area padding on mobile, preventing buttons from resting on edge radii.
- **Interactive Vertical Sinuous Path (Learning Tree)**:
  - Lessons are laid out along a gentle serpentine vertical axis.
  - Nodes step downward at `node-step-vertical` (72px) intervals, alternating laterally across `-node-offset-lateral`, `0`, and `+node-offset-lateral` from the center line.
  - Dotted track connectors use 4px rounded strokes rendered in `#E2D9CC`.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `mobile`: `< 640px` (Main learning view, thumb-zone optimized with primary CTAs pinned in a bottom floating dock).
  - `tablet`: `640px - 1024px` (Path centers to a 480px max-width spine with vocabulary drawers appearing in complementary side sheets).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred drop shadows for functional controls, adopting instead a **crisp 3D mechanical bevel model** to maximize tactile affordance:

- **Tactile Level 1 (Cards & Standard Modules)**:
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: 2px solid `#EDE5D8`
  - Bottom Extrusion Shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #E2D7C5`
- **Tactile Level 2 (Interactive Buttons & Node Chips)**:
  - Resting: Base surface with a solid 4px thick bottom shelf (`0 4px 0 [Color-Bevel-Hex]`).
  - Pressed (`:active`): `transform: translateY(4px); box-shadow: 0 0 0 transparent;`
- **Floating Overlays & Mascot Popups**:
  - Backdrop: Soft warm vignette (`rgba(43, 33, 24, 0.45)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
  - Elevation: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(43, 33, 24, 0.16)`.
- **Streak & Chest Glows**:
  - Soft ambient outer diffusion: `0 0 20px rgba(224, 135, 34, 0.35)` to celebrate milestones and active fire streaks.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **pill-shaped, rounded geometry (Level 3)** that communicates friendliness, safety, and modern approachability:

- **Buttons & Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), inviting touch and reinforcing playful tactility.
- **Cards & Exercise Panels**: `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for outer perimeters, nesting inner target zones at `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px).
- **Learning Map Nodes**: Perfectly circular (`5rem` x `5rem` / 80px) outer housing with concentric inner glyph rings.
- **Text Entry & Choice Tiles**: Rounded rectangles with `1.25rem` (20px) radius to maintain harmony with pill-shaped action triggers.

## Components

### 1. Tactile Push Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Check", "Continue", "Start")**: Pill-shaped with `#1B5E3B` face, `#FFFFFF` bold text, and a `#124027` bottom bevel (4px). On click/touch, translates downward by 4px, flattening the bevel.
- **Secondary / Action ("Skip", "Translate")**: Pill-shaped with `#FFFFFF` face, 2px border `#E08722`, and a `#C77317` bottom shelf. Text is `#E08722`.
- **Destructive ("End Lesson")**: `#D84A38` face with `#9F2B1D` bevel.

### 2. Gamified Path Nodes (The Learning Map)
- **Active / Unlocked Node**: Large circular button (`76px`) featuring Simien Gold (`#E08722`) with an illuminated border, an inner pulsing crown icon, and a floating badge showing current crown level (1-5).
- **Completed Node**: Highland Green (`#1B5E3B`) with an extruded checkmark and golden star halo.
- **Locked Node**: Stone cream `#E8DFD3` with `#BAAFA1` lock icon and 3px grey bevel; completely rigid with no depression on press.

### 3. Stat Badges & Floating HUD
- Located at top of viewport:
  - **Streak Counter**: Pill shape in warm translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`) with orange border, flame icon in `#FF5A1F`, and numeric counter in `label-md`.
  - **Hearts Indicator**: Terracotta `#D84A38` heart icon with real-time numeric counter or infinity icon.
  - **Gem/XP Vault**: Emerald `#10B981` diamond icon with formatted number.

### 4. Choice & Match Tiles (Lesson Engine)
- Used for multiple-choice, audio-matching, and sentence construction:
  - **Default**: White card background, 2px `#E5DDD0` border, 3px `#D5CCBD` bottom rim.
  - **Selected**: Soft gold tint `#FFF7ED`, 2px solid `#E08722`, 3px `#C47318` bottom rim.
  - **Correct State**: Soft mint `#E8F8F0`, 2px `#1B5E3B` border, celebrating with small particle confetti.
  - **Incorrect State**: Soft blush `#FDF0EE`, 2px `#D84A38` border, triggering a soft horizontal shake animation.

### 5. Mascot Callout Bubble
- The mascot (an expressive Ethiopian Walia Ibex or Ethiopian Wolf) anchors key instruction frames.
- Callouts emerge via a rounded speech bubble (`rounded-2xl` with a directional triangle anchor pointing directly to the mascot).
- The bubble uses `#FFFFFF` fill, 2px `#E2D8C9` stroke, and dynamic speaker audio chips that learners can tap to replay slow-speed audio.
